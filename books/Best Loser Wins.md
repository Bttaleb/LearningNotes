### Financial Market Bargain
If price is falling, my mind associates falling prices with *value* and *becoming cheap*
If I decide to act on impulse, one of two things will happen:
1. My feeling of value is confirmed. The market begins to rise
2. My feeling of value is not confirmed. The market continues to fall
If I buy with no good reason other than the impulse to say "the market is cheap", I will lose if the market continues lower
If I buy, and the market continues higher, I will eventually lose because I have taught my mind that it is okay to catch a falling knife
"I have created a pattern in my mind that associated buying falling asset prices with pleasure, because I had success with it at some point"
**There are no bargains in the financial markets**
